About me

I'm an Artificial Intelligence and Data Management student at Badr University in Assiut, currently completing the Machine Learning track at DEPI. I work in Python, and I start where good models start: understanding and cleaning the data.

My approach is simple. Look at the data first, build the simplest model that can work, and measure it honestly. In my projects that has meant predicting calories burned with Linear, Ridge, and Lasso regression, grouping employees with K-Means, and reaching 98.9% accuracy predicting student performance.

I also share what I learn. I help fellow students with Python, data analysis, and machine learning, which keeps my own fundamentals sharp.
